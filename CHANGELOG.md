## [v0.2.0](https://github.com/AlekSi/hardcache/releases/tag/v0.2.0) (2025-12-07)

## What's Changed

* Windows support.
* Support continuous trimming with `hardcache local trimd`.
* Support total disk size percentage: `hardcache local trim(d) --max-size=10%`.
